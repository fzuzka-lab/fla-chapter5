Make it Clear, Specific, and Vivid. It must stir emotion and require real effort. Not just a number — how do you want to feel, move, and live?

Question 8 of 12
What obstacles, setbacks, or doubts do you expect on the swim to your Island?

The chapter says to expect obstacles, fatigue, and doubt. Naming them now is part of the strategy.

Naming it doesn't make it more likely to happen — it makes you more prepared when it does.
Question 9 of 12
What story have you been telling yourself that has held you back — and what is the new story you want to write?

"Rewrite Your Story. Reprogram Daily." The old story isn't the truth — it's just what you've been rehearsing.

The new version doesn't have to feel true yet. Write it anyway.
Question 10 of 12
What is one standard in your life you are willing to shift this week to reduce resistance on the swim?

Environment, community, inner circle, what you listen to, where you go. One shift. This week.

Standards cut resistance. Small shifts compound fast.

A win doesn't have to be scale-related. Anything counts.
